Projects

Options Pricing Engine AI-Assisted
Multi-model derivatives pricing library with five interoperable engines: Black-Scholes closed-form with analytical Greeks, a CRR binomial tree (European and American), Monte Carlo with antithetic and control variates plus pathwise Greeks, a Crank-Nicolson PDE solver verified at second-order spatial convergence, and Heston stochastic volatility via Gil-Pelaez inversion, benchmarked to Albrecher et al. (2007) Table 2 to four significant figures. 53 passing pytest checks covering put-call parity, characteristic-function identities, and Black-Scholes-limit degeneracy.
In other words Five independent implementations of the same quantity, cross-validated against each other and against published reference values, with automated tests for checking identities that must hold.
Python, NumPy, SciPy, Stochastic Calculus, PDE Methods, Monte Carlo, pytest
Equity Premium Forecasting — Rapach, Strauss & Zhou (2010)
Out-of-sample reproduction of the Rapach-Strauss-Zhou combination-forecast framework: expanding-window OLS across 15 macroeconomic predictors, five combination schemes (mean, median, trimmed mean, and DMSPE at two discount factors), each evaluated against the historical-average benchmark. Matched the published Table 1 for 14 of 15 individual predictors; combination forecasts delivered an out-of-sample R² of 3.6%, reproducing the paper's central result that predictors which fail individually can succeed in aggregate.
Methodology The model never sees data from after the point it forecasts, and every result is measured against a simple benchmark rather than reported on its own. We validate by checking the output against their published table.
R, Time Series, Out-of-Sample Evaluation, Forecast Combination
Volatility Surface Lab AI-Assisted In Progress
A toolkit for constructing and calibrating implied volatility surfaces from market option chains. Built so far: a schema-validated data pipeline with composable liquidity filters over committed SPY chain snapshots, and implied-volatility extraction by Brent's method. Project not finished.
Data Handling Ingesting third-party market data through validation and filters before any modeling happens.
Python, NumPy, SciPy, pandas

Research & Publications

Graduate

  • Probabilistic Recognition of Almost Primes by Machine Learning
    With Y. Ye. Working Paper, 2026
    Extending earlier semiprime-detection work with K. H. Wang and Y. Ye to seven r-almost prime detection problems (r = 2,…,8) under the natural distribution, we benchmark random forests against logistic regression using integer-vector encodings generated and trained with PySpark on the Argon HPC cluster, across a grid of N encoding configurations at scales from 2⁴⁴ to 2¹⁷⁶ beat the random-guessing baseline at every r, with random forests winning at the hard low-r end and logistic regression taking the middle of the range. Feature attribution localizes the entire signal in divisibility by small primes.

Undergraduate

  • Failed Zero Forcing Numbers of Trees and Circulant Graphs
    With K. Rubi, J. Terrazas, R. Flórez, D. A. Narayan. Theory and Applications of Graphs, Vol. 11, Iss. 1, Art. 5, 2025
    We investigate the failed zero forcing number F(G) — the largest size of a vertex set that does not force the entire graph under the zero forcing rule. For trees, we prove the lower bound F(T) ≥ α(T) − 1 relating F to the independence number, and we determine F(G) exactly for several families of 4- and 6-regular circulant graphs C_n{1,k}, C_n{1,2}, C_n{1,3}, and C_n{1,2,3} using extremal constructions and pigeonhole arguments.
    PDF
  • All Graphs with a Failed Zero Forcing Number of Two
    With K. Rubi, J. Terrazas, D. A. Narayan. Symmetry, 13, 2221, 2021
    We completely characterize the graphs G with failed zero forcing number F(G) = 2, resolving a problem posed by Fetcie, Jacob, and Saavedra in 2015. The characterization identifies exactly 15 such graphs, and we prove that every graph on at least 7 vertices satisfies F(G) ≥ 3 via a reduction argument that rules out pendant-V and pendant-K_3 obstructions.
