Research

My current work is on log-correlated fields and Gaussian multiplicative chaos, in two halves. The first is arithmetic: computing the covariance of the logarithms of two Dedekind zeta functions on the critical line, where the leading term counts the L-function constituents two fields in a tower share. The second is estimation: finding where multifractal exponents measured from a finite record stop being trustworthy, and which of the two documented breakdowns moves with record length.

Before that, my first graduate project asked how much of an integer's multiplicative structure machine learning can recover from congruence data alone. It returned a null result, and tracing why is the part worth reading.
